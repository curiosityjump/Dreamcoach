# Dream Coach — product brief (condensed)

Dream Coach helps people identify their dreams, take meaningful action toward them,
reflect on their lives, and see their progress over time. It is also the
professional tool for Dream Coaches running one-on-one sessions and for the
Director overseeing the Dream Team.

Roles: **Dreamers**, **Dream Coaches**, **Director**. Same connected system; each
role gets an experience designed around what they need. Dreams belong to the
dreamer; coaches help, they don't own.

Feel: for dreamers a personal space for reflection and momentum — encouraging
without being childish, sophisticated without being complicated, personal without
being invasive, never punishing. For coaches an excellent professional tool that
removes admin work. For the Director a powerful but approachable dashboard.

Dreamer: four dreams (6–12 months, during the program, 3–5 years, lifetime —
labels come from the program), progress 0–100% with history and notes; action
items (add, edit, complete, archive, review, link to dream/CARE, mark private);
CARE Wheel of Life (Contribution: personal/professional growth; Alignment:
personal values/higher purpose; Relationships: others/environment; Energy:
physical/emotional vitality) rated 0–10 any time, any subset; Commitment and
Satisfaction 0–10; journaling attached to ratings or standalone; historical views
per category and per dream mixing numbers and words.

Privacy: any entry can be private; private entries are visible only to the
dreamer, absent everywhere else with no trace. Hide Private is instant; Show
Private requires the password again.

Coach: caseload dashboard; per-dreamer views; live one-on-one workspace modeled on
the existing spreadsheet (months side by side) following the sequence: CARE
ratings → dream progress → review previous actions → commitment → new focus and
actions → satisfaction. Session entries update the shared record; self check-ins
stay distinguishable.

Director: team overview (coaches, caseloads, participation, activity, outcomes,
trends), all dreamers, drill-down, also runs sessions. Sound metrics, no misleading
rankings; private data never included.
